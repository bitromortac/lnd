# Invoices Orchestrate Payment Receipt

Invoices act as the fundamental domain entities for orchestrating the receipt of
funds on the Lightning Network. They define the specific conditions under which
an incoming payment is considered valid, including the required amount,
cryptographic identifiers, and temporal constraints. By tracking the state of
incoming Hash Time-Locked Contracts (HTLCs), they securely govern when the
cryptographic preimage should be revealed.

## Architecture
The invoice subsystem manages the creation, storage, and in-memory coordination
of payment intents.

- **Data model:** [Invoice data model](202603250831-invoice-data-model.md)
  defines the cryptographic material and constraints.
- **Storage:** [Invoice database
  storage](202603250839-invoice-database-storage.md) durably persists invoices
  in a SQL database.
- **Coordination:** [Invoice registry](202603250838-invoice-registry.md)
  synchronizes incoming HTLC events with their corresponding stored invoices.
- **Creation:** [Invoice creation flow](202603250833-invoice-creation-flow.md)
  persists intents and generates payment requests.

## Settlement and State
The lifecycle of an invoice strictly dictates how it transitions in response to
network events.

- **State machine:** [Invoice state
  machine](202603250832-invoice-state-machine.md) progresses an invoice from
  open to accepted, and finally to settled or canceled.
- **Settlement:** [Invoice settlement
  flow](202603250837-invoice-settlement-flow.md) transitions the contract to its
  final state by verifying HTLCs.
- **Hold invoices:** [Hold invoices](202603250834-hold-invoices.md) decouple
  HTLC acceptance from preimage revelation.
- **Expiration:** [Hold invoice
  expiration](202603250835-hold-invoice-expiration.md) prevents forced channel
  closures when hold invoices remain unresolved.
- **AMP:** [Atomic Multi-Path invoices](202603250841-amp-invoices.md) allow
  multiple distinct payments to fulfill a single invoice dynamically.

## External Interfaces
Applications interact with the invoice subsystem to monitor state and intercept
incoming payments.

- **Subscriptions:** [Invoice
  subscriptions](202603250840-invoice-subscriptions.md) provide unidirectional
  streams over RPC for real-time updates.
- **Interception:** [Invoice acceptor](202603250836-invoice-acceptor.md) acts as
  a programmatic gatekeeper for custom HTLC validation.
