# Hold Invoices Decouple Acceptance From Final Settlement

Hold invoices fundamentally alter the traditional Lightning payment flow by
allowing a node to accept an incoming payment without immediately claiming the
associated funds. Standard invoices generate and store the preimage at creation;
when the required HTLCs arrive, the invoice immediately transitions to a settled
state. Hold invoices instead create the invoice using only the payment hash,
leaving the node ignorant of the underlying preimage.

When an incoming payment satisfies the constraints of a hold invoice, the state
transitions to **Accepted**, but the underlying HTLCs remain locked in limbo.
The receiving node cannot claim the funds because it does not know the preimage,
and the payer cannot route the funds elsewhere because the HTLC is still active
and held by the receiver.

This deferred settlement allows an external system, often listening via [invoice
subscriptions](202603250840-invoice-subscriptions.md), to orchestrate complex
out-of-band logic. An application might wait for a physical good to ship or for
another cross-chain transaction to confirm before injecting the preimage back
into the node to finally settle the hold invoice. This enables conditional
payments where finality is determined strictly outside the core protocol logic.
