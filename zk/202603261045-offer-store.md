# Offer Store Persists Long-Lived BOLT 12 Offers

BOLT 12 introduces a concept with no BOLT 11 analog: a persistent, reusable
offer that can generate many invoices over its lifetime. An offer store is the
subsystem responsible for creating, persisting, looking up, and expiring these
offers. It sits alongside the existing [invoice database](lnd/202603250839-invoice-database-storage.md)
but manages a fundamentally different lifecycle — offers are long-lived
templates, not one-shot payment intents.

When an onion message carrying an `invoice_request` arrives, the receiver must
locate the matching offer, verify the request's fields against it, and confirm
the offer has not expired or been disabled. The [invoice request reader
requirements](bolts/202603251330-bolt-12-invoice-request-reader-requirements.md)
mandate that every field from the offer reflected in the request must match
exactly. Without a durable store, the node cannot perform this validation
because offers are stateless on the wire — the issuer must remember what it
published.

The store also enables administrative operations: listing active offers,
disabling an offer without deleting it (so in-flight requests can still settle),
and tracking how many invoices an offer has generated. This is especially
relevant for quantity-limited offers where the [offer writer requirements](bolts/202603251315-bolt-12-offer-writer-requirements.md)
allow capping `offer_quantity_max`.

The offer store will be a dedicated SQL table, not an extension of the existing
invoice schema. Offers have a distinct lifecycle (create once, match many times)
and their own fields (`offer_issuer`, `offer_paths`, currency, quantity limits)
that do not belong on the invoice row. BOLT 12 invoices will reference the
originating offer via a foreign key, linking the two tables without conflating
their concerns.
